## Contrarian Take (Required by ExtractWisdom)

Miller's bull-taming aesthetic risks romanticizing proximity to danger as *always* stylish — but not every bull should be hugged; some should be left in the field. The matador's 4,000-year ritual is also a history of goring, and modern cool that "wears chaos like a belt" can become a macho performance that confuses self-conquest with self-exposure theater — the hipster who *does* hug peril without competence is just a matador in a silly costume with a real bull. The missing discernment is *which* bull is yours to tame (your shadow) vs. *which* chaos is not yours to wear (someone else's trauma, a market's volatility, a bull that's actually a bear). Grace under pressure without wisdom is just slow-motion recklessness.
