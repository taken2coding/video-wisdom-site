## Contrarian Take (Required by ExtractWisdom)

The canticle's "stop blaming the bird" is wise, but sometimes the bird *is* the problem — a diseased bird, a palace pigeon, a system that *does* foul the house. "They revealed what I was" can sanctify any humiliation as revelation, when sometimes being made a bird *is* an injustice, not a diagnosis. The king who keeps the harmless part of his crown may just be clinging to a story while the house rots because no one will call the exterminator.
