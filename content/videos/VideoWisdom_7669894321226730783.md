1. **Prison precedes palace.** Mythologically and psychologically, confinement comes before sovereignty; treasure is *found* in prison.
2. **You reside in your head, not your address.** Prison or palace, the sunset is identical — interiority is the real residence.
3. **"Jail time" as tuition.** Every dream demands willing exile and willingness to look a fool (David drooling at Gath, Dantès in the cell).
4. **David's true coronation is restraint.** Not Goliath (strength), but sparing Saul's hem (restraint) proves the king's heart trained in caves.
5. **Odysseus test: palace-as-prison.** Anyone can endure rough seas (barbarian), only a king endures humiliation at home without breaking — chastising his heart to endure.
6. **Amleth's timing law.** Thrall = vengeance at once, coward = never, king = between now and never — mastery is timed restraint.
7. **Übermensch = savage with manners.** Master physique + slave interiority; regal composure is brute strength governed by privation-trained restraint.

## Mental Models

- **Prison→Palace Inversion:** Treasure is not before prison, but *inside* it. No prison, no palace.
- **Residence Model:** Location = head (interior), not building (exterior). Sunset is constant; viewer is variable.
- **Restraint as Coronation:** Kingship test = what you *don't* do (hem, not throat; spit, not sword).
- **Chronos of Vengeance:** Now (thrall) — *King* — Never (coward). Timing is the virtue.
- **King-in-Rags:** Exterior fool/beggar/slave signals interior king; exterior king with no interior is dunce.
- **Harald Hardrada Loop:** Wood-to-wood obscurity → Byzantine exile → 11 coffins → last Viking king. Exile is the compounding.

## Contrarian Take (Required by ExtractWisdom)

Miller romanticizes privation as the *only* path to kingship, but not all exile is ennobling — some is just trauma without a container, and not all kings are forged in caves. Inherited kings (and modern stewards who receive healthy culture) can be legitimate without 13 years of hollowing, and "willing jail time" without discernment becomes performative suffering — the hustle-cult that confuses self-punishment with self-control. The missing guardrail is *chosen* privation with a telos vs. *imposed* privation with no exit: Dantès had Faria and a map, David had Samuel's anointing, Odysseus had *nostos*. Without a *why* and a *when*, exile doesn't build interiority, it just breaks people.
