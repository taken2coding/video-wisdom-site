## Contrarian Take (Required by ExtractWisdom)

Miller's "past is up for grabs" risks gaslighting — some past *did* happen, with witnesses and scars, and telling yourself "everybody got amnesia, so it never happened" can become denial that bypasses accountability and grief. Not every punk/loser/bitch story is a hysteresis error to be re-narrated; some are accurate descriptions of pattern that need skill, amends, or healing, not just a new story at 2am. The past where the future is won still needs to be *true* enough to act on.
