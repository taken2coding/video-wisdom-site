## Contrarian Take (Required by ExtractWisdom)

The canticle's "I write the laws myself now, nobody can tell" is chillingly competent, but it risks normalizing the kept white sign and the silent cave as legitimate kingship — sometimes the man who keeps the sign *should* have returned it, and the laws he writes *are* distinguishable, and people *do* suffer for the difference even if they can't name it. The spiral shell trick is clever, but finding everyone via ants and honey is still surveillance, not wisdom — and being read in a name's time may be less about being known and more about being filed.
