## Contrarian Take (Required by ExtractWisdom)

Miller glorifies the white-knuckle, Herzogian dream — boat over mountain, opera in jungle — as *the* proof of being human, but not every dream deserves to let it kill you; some dreams *should* die in the jungle because they were vanity, not vocation. The film crew's suffering behind Fitzcarraldo (and *Aguirre*, *Mosquito Coast*) wasn't noble to those exploited; the burden of dreams can become a burden *on others*. The missing ethic is *whose* ice is melting, *whose* back is breaking, and *whose* jungle is being paraded through — the dream that separates men from cattle can also separate men from their neighbors if the mask of art is used to aestheticize harm.
