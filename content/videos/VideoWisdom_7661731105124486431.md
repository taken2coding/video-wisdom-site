## Key Insights (Distilled)

1. **Maldon as hinge of Viking Age.** 991 sits 200 years after Lindisfarne (793) and ~75 years before Stamford Bridge (1066) — a middle-age stress test of Saxon honor vs. Norse opportunism at the Blackwater causeway.
2. ***Ofermod* — costly chivalry.** Byrhtnoth's letting Vikings cross is not just honor but *ofermod* (over-boldness/hubris). Needless nobility can be noble and disastrous.
3. **Tolkien's Totta/Tidwald lens.** The poem survives as a fragment; Tolkien (and *The Homecoming of Beorhtnoth*) makes it legible by following the scavengers, not just the earls — war seen from the aftermath.
4. **Old English as battle psychology.** *Hige sceal þe heardra...* ("Will shall be the stouter, heart the bolder, spirit the greater, as our strength lessens") is not bravado but anti-fragile will — spirit inversely proportional to power.
5. **England's second Maldon — attrition wins.** The later victory over Norway comes not via heroic charge but long, costly, inch-by-inch war of attrition — same verse, applied over years.
6. **X-Ray meter as modern *ofermod*.** In Mortal Kombat, you fill X-Ray by *taking* combos (*poop poop poop*) — losing resources builds the one-shot that winners never get to charge.
7. **Inversion: less strength = more will.** Miller's lived translation: "As my strength lessens, my will becomes greater" — taking lumps is not losing, it's charging.

## Mental Models

- **Ofermod Test:** Is this honor or over-boldness? Needless nobility can cost more than tribute.
- **Causeway Decision:** Hold the bridge (pick them off) vs. let them cross to fight "fairly." When is fairness folly?
- **Old English Will Formula:** Will ∝ 1/Power. As power ↓, stout will must ↑ to compensate.
- **Attrition vs. Assault:** One battle lost heroically vs. war won by inches over years — choose your timeline.
- **X-Ray Economy:** Damage taken → meter filled → finisher unlocked. Losing *is* resource accrual if you stay in fight.
- **Totta/Tidwald View:** History from scavengers, not kings — what does the aftermath say about your "noble" choice?

## Contrarian Take (Required by ExtractWisdom)

Miller's X-Ray romanticizes taking damage as *always* charging will, but *ofermod* is literally the poem's critique of Byrhtnoth: his will *did* get bolder as power lessened, and it still got his men killed and forced tribute — will without wisdom is just pride with better poetry. Mortal Kombat's X-Ray only matters if you *survive* to use it; some lumps don't fill a meter, they just concuss. The missing check is *which* lumps are X-Ray fuel (chosen attrition, bounded causeway) vs. *which* are just self-inflicted *ofermod* where holding the bridge *was* the courage — and walking away was the greater will.
