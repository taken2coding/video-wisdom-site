## Contrarian Take (Required by ExtractWisdom)

The hermit's "you move, time doesn't" is elegant, but risks dissociating — if past and future are already here, why act? The thin present as mere attention-touch can become quietist bypass where walking is devalued and holding everything at once becomes an excuse to never take the next step. Sometimes time *does* move forward as entropy, decay, and deadline — the gods' whole-path view is not available to mortals who must still walk, and mistaking the map for the road can be as paralyzing as mistaking walking for road.
