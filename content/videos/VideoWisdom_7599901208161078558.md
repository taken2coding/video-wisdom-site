- **Conquer vs. Carry:** Are you conquering or carrying? River resists the first, yields to the second.
- **Watched Ripening:** What ripens when felt watched? Use witnessed presence as ripener.
- **Empty-Handed Arrival:** What does earth read when you arrive empty-handed? Bring nothing to be trusted.
- **Escort, Don't Harvest:** Is this yours to harvest or to escort? If not yours, escort.
- **Severity as Weight:** If you look severe, check if you're carrying abundance — severity may be heaviness, not harshness.

## Contrarian Take (Required by ExtractWisdom)

The canticle's "I was born to carry, not conquer" beautifies the ferrier as humble escort, but some river crossings *do* require conquest — dam, bridge, or harvest — and "escort what grows does not belong to me" can become a way to never claim, tend, or steward what *does* belong to you. The ferrier who never harvests may watch fruit rot while waiting to be severe; sometimes abundance needs a harvester, not just a carrier, and the river that doesn't resist may be inviting you to *take*.
